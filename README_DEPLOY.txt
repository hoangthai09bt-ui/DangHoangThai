WEBSITE ADA LOVELACE - BAN DA SUA

1. Giai nen ZIP nay. Cac file HTML (index.html, cuocdoi.html, ...) va thu muc assets/ nam cung cap.
2. Tai len GitHub repository sao cho index.html nam o thu muc goc ma GitHub Pages dang xuat ban; khong de ca thu muc ada_site_fixed long ben trong.
3. Dam bao thu muc assets/ duoc tai len day du. Cac anh trong thu vien su dung duong dan tuong doi assets/ada-XX.jpg.
4. Vao Settings > Pages de xac nhan nhanh deploy, sau do mo lai website va nhan Ctrl+F5.
5. Video YouTube co the bi chan boi mang/trinh duyet; trang co lien ket du phong de mo danh sach video.
6. Truoc khi nop, mo thu ca 8 trang, bam tat ca muc menu, kiem tra anh va video tren dien thoai/may tinh. Can kiem tra lai cac nguon tham khao va bo sung nguon anh goc neu giao vien yeu cau ghi nguon rieng.
